Stud Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts wall studs and plate boards with division and rounding-up: the studs a wall takes at a spacing, the plate boards, the cost of the boards, and the bundles. It uses one named constant: 12 inches in a foot. The wall length, the spacing, the extra studs, the board length, the prices and the bundle size are yours to measure and read off your own plans and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your wall and does not know your local code, your corners, your openings or your framing plan, and it gives no verdict that a count is enough or that a wall is built to hold. Every other number here is your own entry.

The studs truth

Studs: the wall in inches over the spacing, rounded up, plus 1 for the end, plus your extra studs.

The plates truth

Plates: the wall length times the plate count, divided by the board length, rounded up, with the feet left.

The boards truth

Boards: the studs plus the plate boards, at their price.

The bundles truth

Bundles: the boards divided by the bundle size, rounded up, with the boards left.
